NEET.info Counsellor Assist

Every number, traced to a notice.

A working extract of the CGPRR case: 28 synthetic documents across two counselling sessions, hybrid retrieval, a numeric guard that blocks any figure not present in the cited chunk, and a resume matcher whose score is provably blind to employment gaps.

    Pick a question on the left. Answers below are generated by the prototype on load — nothing here is written by hand.

    The numeric guard

    Type an answer and pick the notice it cites. Any figure that does not appear in that source is flagged. This is the same rule the Python prototype enforces — ported to the browser so you can break it yourself.

    Roofline

    Worst hour
    
      
    70% of a day's traffic in four hours, six turns per chat. "Design" assumes a 2,200-token context cap with a 60% prefix-cache hit.

        The finding that matters: 75% of the cost is two people. The GPU is not the expensive part
        of this plan, and cloud is not cheaper — it is break-even at 2.91 million chats a year.
