    KV cache and concurrency
    
          
    Per token: 2 × layers × KV heads × head dim × bytes. 43.2 GB usable after a 10% runtime reserve.
    
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Roofline
    
          
    Batch-of-one decode is not capacity: at batch N the limit is bytes moved per step.
